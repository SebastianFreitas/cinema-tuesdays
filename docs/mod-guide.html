Cinema Tuesdays
mod guide

How to run movie night with The Legend, the bot that replaced the Google Sheet. People suggest and rate with slash commands; mods run the weekly vote. Everything appears on the website within about 10 minutes.

Tuesdays 21:15 movie night Group α / ω take turns 10 suggestions each Mod commands need Manage Messages

The weekly cycle

  1. Any time · everyone

    People fill the Movie List

    /suggest adds a movie (it searches TMDB as you type, so everyone gets the same poster and year). /unsuggest removes one. Max 10 each.

  2. Before the weekend · mod

    Open the vote

    /poll open curated:Seven Samurai
    • The bot knows whose week it is and takes one random suggestion from each member of that group.
    • Anyone whose pick won last time is skipped (it says so on the vote).
    • curated is the Cultured Snobs' pick, always the last option. Leave it out if there isn't one.
    • Each movie gets a card with its poster and a button. One vote per person; clicking another movie moves your vote, clicking the same one removes it.
  3. Before Tuesday · mod

    Close the vote

    /poll close
    • The bot announces the winner. A tie is broken at random.
    • Whoever suggested the winner sits out their group's next vote.
    • Then post the download in #link as usual.
  4. After the screening · mod

    Mark it as watched

    /watched
    • With no options it uses the vote winner and today's date. For anything else: /watched movie:… date:…
    • This adds it to the website and unlocks /rate for that movie.
  5. After the screening · everyone

    Everyone rates

    /rate movie:Seven Samurai score:8.1 remark:…
    • Scores go from 0.0 to 10.0 and are posted in #rate-movies. Running it again changes your score.
    • /ratings movie:… shows everyone's scores for one movie.

The two groups

The groups alternate each week. Only the current group's suggestions go into the vote.

The vote for 20 Oct ran as a normal Discord poll (Group α). So the first vote in the bot, for 27 Oct, is Group ω: use /poll open group:Group ω that one time. After that the bot alternates on its own.

Special weeks and mistakes

Birthweek

/poll pick member:@them movie:…

The birthday person's pick replaces the vote. If a vote is already open it gets cancelled. It doesn't use up a group's turn and nobody has to sit out.

Opened a vote by accident, or for the wrong group

/poll cancel

Deletes the open vote and its votes. It doesn't count as anyone's turn. Open it again with /poll open group:… if needed.

Choosing the options yourself

/poll open movies:Rango; Stalker; Coherence

Skips the rotation and uses those Movie List titles, separated by ;. Use per_person:2 instead to give each group member two movies in a normal vote.

Someone joins the server

They can /suggest straight away. Add them to a group with /group set, or they won't appear in votes.
