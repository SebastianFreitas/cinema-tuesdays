## Old #start-here (posted by the moderators, edited)

*The top of this message (anything above "Channel Structure") wasn't in the screenshot.*

> ## Channel Structure
> #start-here, #movie-night-cartaz and #link — These act as a notice board. Only moderators can send messages in these channels.
> #general — Free for any discussion.
> #rate-movies — Suggested format: movie name + score (0.0/10.0) + a short remark if you want. For longer discussions, use #general.
>
> ## Session Dynamics
> **Weekly Schedule**: Usually Tuesday at 21:15 (WET/ UTC +0).
> **The Movie**: We always provide a link with the movie for everyone to download ( #link ).
> **Synchronization**: Since we watch the movies on our own computers, it is mandatory to install [Syncplay](https://syncplay.pl/download/)(which works with [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/)).
>
> ## Suggestions and Voting
> **Suggestions**: You can leave your movie recommendations in the Movie List (link) document. There is a strict limit of 10 suggestions per person.
> **Votes**: The movie of the week is always chosen by voting in #movie-night-cartaz . Only those who plan to attend should vote. If something comes up unexpectedly, you can remove your vote or talk to a moderator.
> **Scores**: Review scores are recorded in the Movie Ratings (link) document.
>
> ## How to use Syncplay
> **Server address:** syncplay.pl:8995
> **Username:** your nickname
> **Default Room:** MovieTuesday
>
> After running Syncplay, two windows will open: one for VLC and one for Syncplay.
> In the VLC window, load the movie file; you can drag the file into the window, or use Media > Open File.
> In the Syncplay window, click "Ready" when you are set.

> # Rotation of the Polls
> Hello @everyone, since there is now an increased number of people suggesting movies every week, we, the @Cultured Snobs, have decided to
> **1.** Separate all the people suggesting movies into two groups (Group **α** and group **ω**) and have those two groups in a biweekly rotation, so that there are fewer movies every week to pick from and facilitate the selection. If none of the movies that you have suggested are in this week's poll trust that they will be in next week's poll.
> **2.** If one of the movies you suggested is selected for **Movie Tuesdays**, then your suggestions for the next rotation will not be considered. This is to prevent the same people always getting their suggestions selected, and to give others the opportunity to have their movie suggestions selected.
> **3.** This rule technically already existed, but it wasn't written down. If it is the week of your birthday (your birthweek) you can have the pick of the movie for that week and override the poll. Do inform the @Cultured Snobs if that is the case.
> **4.** Additionally, in our infinite wisdom, we, the @Cultured Snobs, will effort to add a curated option for the movie poll every week, it will always be the last option on the poll.
>
> That is all, things will continue as routine, don't forget to vote on the poll. Feel free to give suggestions and comments on the #general chat. Distribution of group **α**(alpha) and group **ω**(omega) will be handled shortly.
