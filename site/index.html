      Fair ranking

      Every movie ranked as if the same people had scored it. Each score has that person's usual lean taken out first (how far above or below the others they score on the same movies), so a movie isn't helped by generous scorers or held back by tough ones. Leans from only a few scores count less. Move: places gained or lost against the plain average. Weighted: a second, separate ranking where movies with few scores are pulled toward the overall average, so a handful of 9s can't top the table. Hover or tap a column name for what it means.
